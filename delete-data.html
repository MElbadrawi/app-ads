Delete data for Mosaic Bloom

Tile Sort: Mosaic Bloom
Android package: com.eaeteam.tilecraft
Developer: EAE Softwares · Updated 29 October 2026

The game has no user account or cloud-save service. Local puzzle progress and Google-hosted advertising, statistics and crash records are different data. Clearing one does not automatically erase the other.

1. Delete progress on your device

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Mosaic Bloom → Storage.
  2. Choose Clear storage / Clear data. The wording depends on your Android device.

This removes saved levels, stars, best move counts, coins, earned boosters, the unfinished puzzle, sound settings and privacy choices on that installation. Uninstalling also removes the game's local data. EAE Softwares does not keep a cloud recovery copy or an additional retention period for this local progress. This cannot be undone through the game.

2. Stop future optional reports

Open the game's Home → Settings → Privacy & data. Switch off Play statistics and Crash reports separately. These start off by default. Turning Crash reports off also asks the SDK to delete unsent reports. Turning reporting off does not recall records Google already received.

3. Request deletion of identifiable records

Email eaesoftwares@gmail.com with the subject Mosaic Bloom data deletion.

  • Say which data you want deleted: optional play statistics, crash reports, or another privacy request.
  • Include the game package above, the approximate dates of use and the app version if available.
  • If you already know a relevant app-instance or installation identifier, you may include it to help locate technical records. Do not send passwords, payment information, a government ID or your device's IMEI.

We review the request, explain what can be located, and handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and the relevant service's capabilities. The game does not send an account email or name with telemetry, so an email address alone may not identify a technical record. We may need further information to identify a relevant record, and cannot promise to erase records that cannot reliably be associated with the request.

Records that can be removed include identifiable optional Analytics for Firebase or Crashlytics records that we can locate and request the provider to delete. Local progress is removed directly using step 1. Provider-held advertising, security or legally required records may be handled separately by Google; they are not erased by clearing the game.

4. Advertising data and retention

Use Home → Settings → Privacy & data → Ad privacy choices where available, and Android's advertising/privacy controls to reset or delete the advertising ID or change available choices. AdMob still processes data needed to serve and measure permitted ads and prevent fraud.

Google-hosted analytics, crash and advertising records follow the relevant product's retention settings, deletion cycles, backup practices and legal requirements. These records are not stored in an EAE game-account database. EAE does not retain an additional server copy of local puzzle progress. Disabling optional reporting stops future optional collection; any previously received records remain subject to those provider policies and a valid deletion request.

Provider details: Firebase privacy and security, Google Analytics data retention, and Google retention and deletion practices.
