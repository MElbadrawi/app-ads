Metro Flow: City Routes

Privacy Policy

Updated 24 October 2026. Developer: EAE Softwares. Android package: com.eaeteam.metroflow.

Metro Flow works offline and does not require an account. Your level progress, settings, rewards and train collection are stored on your device. Recovery codes contain game progress and preferences; you control where you keep or share them.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and ads between completed levels. Google may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve, measure, secure and improve ads. We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request choices where applicable. Ad privacy options in the game let you review available choices. Ads are not required to progress through the campaign.

Optional analytics and crash reporting

Diagnostics are off by default. If enabled in the game's privacy settings, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process gameplay events such as level starts and completions, retries, failure reasons, play duration and booster use, as well as device information, installation identifiers, technical diagnostics and crash reports. You may turn optional diagnostics off in the game. A bounded local gameplay event log supports troubleshooting and is stored only on your device.

Information we do not request

The game does not request contacts, precise device location, microphone, camera, payment information or a player account. We do not sell player profiles or request sensitive personal information.

Providers and retention

Google acts as a service provider under its own terms and may process data internationally. See Google's Privacy Policy, Google partner-site and app information and Firebase privacy information. Provider retention varies by service and configuration. Device data remains until cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to any recovery copy you keep.

Your choices and rights

You can play offline, manage ad privacy choices, disable optional diagnostics and remove local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Keep a recovery code before removing data if you want to preserve progress. For applicable access, correction or deletion requests concerning data associated with the app, contact us; we may ask for the information needed to identify your request.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Parents may contact us about privacy concerns.
